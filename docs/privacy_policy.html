Information Collected

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any Personal Data or usage data. Specifically, the App:

  • has no account or sign-in, and never asks for your name, email address, contacts, or location;
  • contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools;
  • does not use the internet, so nothing it does can be sent off your Device.

Data Stored on Your Device

To let you continue where you left off, the App saves your game progress and settings (the furthest room you have reached, your best time, your selected hero, and your music, sound-effect and vibration settings) in the App's private storage on your own Device. This data stays on your Device, is never sent to me or anyone else, and I have no access to it.

You can delete it at any time by clearing the App's storage in your Device settings or by uninstalling the App.

Device Permissions

The App requests only the vibration permission, which it uses to give haptic feedback during gameplay. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, files, or the internet.
