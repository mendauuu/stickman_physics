## Full description (max 4000)

```
Exams are monsters now. Literally.

Exam Escape: Pixel Brawler is a fast pixel-art brawler set on a university campus that has been overrun by homework goblins, deadline mushrooms and teachers with very real superpowers. Fight your way through three buildings, survive every final exam, and walk out of the last door into a world that is finally yours to explore.

⚔️ FIGHT ROOM BY ROOM
Clear monster halls wave by wave, beat each teacher in a two-round boss fight (Midterm, then the Final Exam), and grab a breather at rest stops. 18 rooms across Building B, Building C and the Main Building, each with its own look, mood and music.

🌳 ESCAPE INTO THE OPEN
Between buildings the walls fall away: battle through sunny campus gardens and a misty pine trail, with layered parallax scenery that moves as you do. Beat the final boss and the doors open onto mountains, sky and freedom.

🧑‍🎓 7 PLAYABLE HEROES
Knight, Swordsman, Mage, Dark Knight, Samurai, Goblin and Umid. Every hero has a different weapon, reach and special power, from a teleporting Iaido slash to a returning blade of hope. Beat teachers to unlock them all.

👹 7 BOSSES WITH REAL POWERS
Each teacher fights with powers from their own subject: Newton's apples, number storms, syntax-error lasers, AI drones, market crashes, email spam and more. And waiting on the roof at midnight is the Grade Reaper, who dashes, teleports and turns out the lights.

🎮 EASY TO PLAY, HARD TO MASTER
• Combo attacks, double jump and a dodge roll with perfect-dodge rewards
• Charge your ⚡ power meter and unleash your hero's special
• Pick perks at rest stops: Green Tea, Sharp Pencil, Energy Drink, New Sneakers and more
• Combo counter, hit-stop and screen shake that make every hit feel great
• Replay any room you've reached and chase your best graduation time

✨ MADE WITH LOVE FOR PIXEL ART
Hand-drawn pixel characters, crisp parallax landscapes, fireflies, falling leaves, birds and rain. Every room has classmates who cheer you on when the monsters are gone.

📴 PLAY ANYWHERE
Works fully offline. No account, no ads, no in-app purchases.

Grab your sword. Class is in session.
```

## Before you publish

- The game uses the Millat Umidi University name and real teachers' names. Get written permission from the university and from each teacher, or rename them, before the app goes public. Google Play can remove apps that use an organisation's identity without authorisation. This listing text already avoids naming them.
- Screenshot 5 shows real teachers' names on the campus map. Swap it for a different screenshot if you rename them.
- Art credits (keep these licences in the repo): characters by Ozzbit Games, CraftPix.net and the other packs in assets/, landscapes by CraftPix.net.
